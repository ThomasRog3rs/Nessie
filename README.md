# Nesse

Nesse is a mobile-first, invite-only app for coordinating house- and pet-sitting arrangements between people who know one another. It gives bookers and sitters a shared place to manage availability, booking requests, agreed handover times, updates, expenses, and cancellations.

Nesse is a scheduling and record-keeping tool—not a public sitter marketplace, payment processor, emergency-response service, or source of legal advice. Authentication and product functionality are still under development.
